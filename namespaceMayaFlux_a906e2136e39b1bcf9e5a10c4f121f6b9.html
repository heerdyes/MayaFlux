template<typename T>
bool MayaFlux::expose_live_indexed ( std::string_view prefix,
const std::shared_ptr< T > & obj )
noexcept

Exposes obj under the first free key of the form "prefix_N".

N starts at 0 and advances past keys already in the arena, so every call site sharing a prefix, such as the overloads of one Creator factory, draws from one sequence and none of them collide.
