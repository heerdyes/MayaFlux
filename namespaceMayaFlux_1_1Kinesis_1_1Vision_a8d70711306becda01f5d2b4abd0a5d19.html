enum class MayaFlux::Kinesis::Vision::ChannelMask : uint8_t
strong

Selects channels of the current image for a multichannel threshold.

Ch0, Ch1 and Ch2 are the first three channels as stored: red, green, blue on an RGBA image, hue, saturation, value after RgbaToHsv. NONE selects the legacy single-channel path, which reads channel 0 only. Multichannel selection is implemented by the GPU backend only.
