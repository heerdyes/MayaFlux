ImageFormat of an image that can be written as one flat block.

The mirror of readable_format() with transfer-destination usage in place of transfer-source: an initialised single-layer 2D image of depth 1, a mapped format that is not combined depth-stencil, and a nonzero base level that fits the image's allocation.

Returns
The format, or nullopt when any requirement is not met.
