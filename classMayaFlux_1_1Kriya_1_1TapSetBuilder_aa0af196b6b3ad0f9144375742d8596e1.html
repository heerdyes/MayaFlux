TapSetBuilder & MayaFlux::Kriya::TapSetBuilder::lag ( double seconds)

Make the current tap trail the write head of a live ring by seconds.

For a stream that is being filled through append_frames in circular mode, for example one made by create_ring and fed by record_into. The tap plays what was written that long ago. It replaces speed and direction, so speed() and repeat_every do not apply to it. Lags shorter than two blocks are raised to two blocks so the tap stays behind the frames being written.
