Tangents of a sampled curve by finite difference, coordinate-major.

Parameters
pointsCoordinate-major, dim * count doubles.
dimCoordinate count per sample, at least 1.
countSample count, at least 2.
tangentsResized to dim * count, coordinate-major.

For curves whose samples did not come straight from CurveEvaluator's analytic derivative, most importantly after reparameterize_planar has resampled by arc length, which leaves analytic tangents attached to the original parameter values. Uniformly spaced samples make the central difference well conditioned; the endpoints take the one sided difference.

Like the analytic tangents, these are directions and not rates.
