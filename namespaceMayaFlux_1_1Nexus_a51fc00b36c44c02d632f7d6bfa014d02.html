Target that feeds an influence of vector fields into a PhysicsOperator as a force.

Parameters
opPhysicsOperator, as a raw pointer from create_operator or a shared_ptr.

Adds one forwarding force field now, and every time the influence runs the target replaces what that field forwards to. Force fields already added to op are left alone. The operator is not retained.
