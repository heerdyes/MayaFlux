198 /**
199 * @brief Non-blocking counterpart of dispatch_sequence.
200 *
201 * Records the identical command buffer and submits it via
202 * ShaderFoundry::submit_async instead of submit_and_wait. Barriers owed to
203 * each stage's hazards are recorded as in dispatch_sequence, including the
204 * one after the final stage, so a caller that does not await the fence
205 * still gets the trailing dependency for anything submitted later.
206 *
207 * Like dispatch_sequence, and unlike dispatch_async, no shader-write to
208 * host-read barrier is recorded. Host reads after the fence has signaled
209 * rely on the fence's own visibility guarantee and on the buffers being
210 * host coherent.
211 *
212 * @return FenceID to poll with ShaderFoundry::is_fence_signaled and to
213 * release once signaled.
214 */
