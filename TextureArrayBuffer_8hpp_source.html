14/**
15 * @class TextureArrayBuffer
16 * @brief A TextureBuffer whose image is a stack of layers, composed in one draw.
17 *
18 * Each layer is a frame of the same size and format, bound to the fragment shader
19 * as a sampler2DArray named textureArray. What the stack means is the shader's
20 * choice: a blend, a median, a slit-scan, a delay map, a difference, a mosaic.
21 * A layer that is not written keeps its last frame, so sources at different rates
22 * can share one array.
23 *
24 * A layer is written from raw pixels, an ImageData or a VKImage, by index or at a
25 * write head that advances on every push_layer. A frame whose extent differs from
26 * the array is dropped, or fitted into the layer with the FitMode chosen by
27 * set_fit. A fit leaves any area it does not cover with the layer's previous
28 * contents. Raw pixels must already match the layer, and are written through host
29 * storage that is uploaded once per cycle. The first VKImage, or an ImageData that
30 * needs converting, moves the buffer to writing each layer straight into the
31 * image, which keeps layers written either way intact. Every such write completes
32 * before the call returns.
33 *
34 * A fit that does not cover the whole layer clears the layer first, so a smaller
35 * part replacing a larger one leaves nothing of it behind. Each layer may have its
36 * own fit through set_layer_fit.
37 *
38 * The shipped shader, texture_array.frag, takes a push constant block of the layer
39 * count, a mode number whose meaning belongs to the shader, and a weight for each
40 * of the first WEIGHT_SLOTS layers. Choose a shader with
41 * setup_rendering({ .fragment_shader = ... }). Extra textures such as a delay map,
42 * or a second array, go in RenderConfig::additional_textures and bind from binding
43 * 2 by the sampler's name. A shader with its own push constants replaces the block
44 * through set_push_constants().
45 *
46 * Two vec4 per layer reach the shader beyond the push constants once
47 * enable_layer_data is called, or set_layer_params is, before the first frame. A
48 * shader reads them with
49 * `layout(set = 1, binding = 0) readonly buffer LayerData { vec4 layerData[]; };`
50 * where layerData[2 * layer] holds the params, which are the caller's to use for
51 * placement, scale, a mask or anything else, and layerData[2 * layer + 1] holds the
52 * timing, which a Chimera fills for the layers it feeds. A shader that does not
53 * declare the buffer must not enable it.
54 *
55 * Rendering, transform and window targeting are those of TextureBuffer. Reading
56 * the buffer back through a texture read returns every layer and is not meant for
57 * this class. Write from the graphics frame clock or the thread that processes the
58 * buffer.
59 */
