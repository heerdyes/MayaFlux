Portal::Graphics::FenceID MayaFlux::Yantra::TextureExecutionContext::dispatch_async ( const input_type & input)
inlinenodiscard

Non-blocking dispatch.

If datum.container holds a TextureContainer it is uploaded and staged before submission. Fence becomes signaled when GPU work completes. Call collect_result() (SCALAR mode) or retrieve the output Datum via execute() (CONTAINER mode) once signaled.

Parameters
inputDataIO whose container field drives optional image staging.
Returns
FenceID to poll with ShaderFoundry::is_fence_signaled.
